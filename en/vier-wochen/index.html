    Four-week path for self-study

    Through all belts in four weeks

    A free plan, no sign-up and no course. One or two belts each week.

    1. 1

      Week 1

      WhiteYellow

      If you practice this week’s techniques, afterwards you have:

      • the questions you have to answer yourself before an important text (No. 1 The Interrogation)
      • your recipient’s objections, while you can still fix them (No. 5 The Opponent in the Mirror)
      • a list of differences between draft and source (No. 6 The Hawk Eye)
      • the reasons your project could fail (No. 7 The View from the End)
    2. 2

      Week 2

      Orange

      If you practice this week’s techniques, afterwards you have:

      • a comparison of quotes with the fine print in one table (No. 9 The Quote Duel)
      • a list of deadlines for your calendar entries (No. 10 The Pitfall)
      • your writing style profile (No. 11 The Shadow Writer)
      • a quiz on a policy, with references (No. 13 The Dojo Quiz)
    3. 3

      Week 3

      GreenBlue

      If you practice this week’s techniques, afterwards you have:

      • your own agent built from your best chat (No. 14 The Imprint)
      • a review table for its instructions (No. 17 The Scroll Check)
      • 20 test questions with the list “not covered” (No. 18 The Belt Exam)
      • a safeguard block for every instruction (No. 19 The Armor)
    4. 4

      Week 4

      BrownBlack

      If you practice this week’s techniques, afterwards you have:

      • a template to pass on real cases anonymized (No. 22 The Invisibility Cloak)
      • an estimate of the order of magnitude of Copilot Credits your agent uses (No. 23 The Credit Compass)
      • an operations sheet with shutdown criteria and a responsible person (No. 24 The Code of Honor)
      • a routine for the monthly check (No. 25 The Night Watch)

    After four weeks you have your own agent with test questions, safeguard block, credit estimate and operations sheet.

    What you need for it is shown in the access line of every technique.

    Prefer to continue with guidance? The live course “Copilot Ninja in 4 Wochen” (in German) at the Power Platform Academy runs over four weeks. The next dates are not set yet, you can join the waiting list on the course page.
