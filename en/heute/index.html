    The TrailNo. 12: Web research with mandatory sources

    • Orange belt
    • No add-on license, in Microsoft Copilot Chat with web search
    • Try it: approx. 10 min

    Management wants an overview of new regulations in your industry. The answer sounds confident, but you cannot see which sentence comes from where.

    Make it yours

    Nothing leaves your browser.

    e.g. your research question, without names or internal figures
    e.g. government websites and vendor documentation

    Prompt to copy

    Goal: Research on the web: [Question, e.g. your research question, without names or internal figures]

Source: Only sources you name with a link. Prefer [Source type, e.g. government websites and vendor documentation].

Expectations:
1. An answer in no more than eight sentences. After each sentence, put the link it comes from.
2. A list “Not supported” with everything you suspect but cannot back up with a source.
3. For each source, the date of the page if given, otherwise “no date”.
If sources contradict each other, show the contradiction instead of resolving it.
