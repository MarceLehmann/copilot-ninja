    License check

    Is Microsoft Copilot free?

    Partly: according to Microsoft, Microsoft Copilot Chat is included without an add-on license if you sign in with a work or school account and your organization has a qualifying Microsoft 365 subscription, for example Business Basic, Business Standard or Business Premium. Copilot Chat (Basic) has no access to Copilot in Word, Excel, PowerPoint and OneNote without an add-on. Microsoft 365 Copilot (Basic) provides standard access in these apps without an add-on, which may be limited at high demand. Microsoft 365 Copilot (Premium or Pro) provides Priority Access and full functionality with an add-on license, including broad organizational data through Graph and Work IQ. The in-product label shows your level; availability depends on your license and tenant configuration.

    Source: Microsoft Learn, copilot/manage and copilot/overview, checked on October 13, 2026. Prices are not listed here, because I have not checked them.

    Not licensing advice. Licenses and prices may have changed since publication. The information is a guide only.

              Without an add-on license, if your subscription is eligible

              • Chat with web search, file upload and image generation, plus drafting and rewriting texts. (Learn: copilot/overview)
              • Agents with instructions and public websites, available by default. (Learn: copilot/agents)
              • Agent Builder in the chat, free for agents with knowledge from the web. (Learn: copilot-studio-experience)
              • Enterprise Data Protection for prompts and responses when signed in with an Entra account, with no admin action. (Learn: copilot/faq)
              • Agents for Word, Excel and PowerPoint directly in the chat, depending on settings and license. (Learn: copilot/faq, as of October 15, 2026)
              • Limited in Outlook: emails, calendar, meetings, chats and a limited set of files. (Learn: copilot/overview)
              • Copilot Studio as a trial: build agents and check them in the test chat. (Learn: requirements-licensing-subscriptions)

              App access, add-on or capacity

              • Copilot in Word, Excel, PowerPoint and OneNote: no access with Copilot Chat (Basic); standard access without an add-on with Microsoft 365 Copilot (Basic), possibly limited at high demand; full functionality and Priority Access with the Microsoft 365 Copilot (Premium or Pro) add-on. Check the in-product label, license and tenant configuration. (Learn: copilot/overview)
              • Automatic access through Microsoft Graph, Work IQ and Semantic Indexing. (Learn: copilot/overview)
              • Priority Access and real-time voice conversation. (Learn: copilot/overview, copilot/faq)
              • Agents with data from SharePoint or Graph connectors: billed by consumption, off by default for Copilot Chat users. (Learn: copilot/agents)
              • Publishing and running an agent from Copilot Studio. Publishing needs capacity (Copilot Credits or pay-as-you-go) and author permission. (Learn: requirements-licensing)
