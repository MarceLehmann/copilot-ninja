    Copilot without an add-on license: every condition with its source

    Here you find the myths, the four levels, every condition word for word and the frequently asked questions, each with source and date. The license check in four questions is on the page before.

    Not licensing advice. Licenses and prices may have changed since publication. The information is a guide only.

    To the license checkEvery condition word for word

    Myth or documented?

    Four statements you have surely heard before.

    Every card has its own link. Pass it on when someone on the team tells the myth again.

    • Myth

      “An Entra account is enough for Copilot Chat.”

      Documented

      An Entra account alone is not an entitlement. Microsoft Copilot Chat is only included at no extra cost if your organization has a qualifying Microsoft 365 subscription.

      Source: Learn: copilot/manage, as of October 13, 2026. Direct link to the Entra account myth

    • Myth

      “You can publish in the Copilot Studio trial.”

      Documented

      In the trial you build agents and check them in the test chat. The trial does not include publishing. Publishing needs capacity (Copilot Credits or pay-as-you-go) and author permission. Microsoft writes: “You can't publish the agent.”

      Source: Learn: requirements-licensing-subscriptions, as of October 13, 2026. Direct link to the trial myth

    • Myth

      “Without a license, Copilot Chat automatically reads all your emails.”

      Documented

      Without a license, Copilot Chat does not access your data automatically through Microsoft Graph. You bring in content yourself, as text or by file upload. In Outlook, Copilot Chat can include emails, calendar, meetings, chats and a limited set of files to a limited extent.

      Source: Learn: microsoft-365-copilot-overview and copilot/overview, as of October 13, 2026. Direct link to the emails myth

    • Myth

      “Web search queries are protected just like your prompts.”

      Documented

      The Enterprise Data Protection commitment applies to prompts and responses. Web search queries go to the Bing service and run under their own data rules. According to Learn, the Data Protection Addendum does not cover them.

      Source: Learn: enterprise-data-protection and copilot/faq, as of October 13, 2026. Direct link to the web search myth

    From easy to hard

    Which level do you need?

    In my trainings I always go from the bottom up. First I ask: does this work with Microsoft Copilot Chat without an add-on license? Is Agent Builder enough? Does it need a license for Microsoft Copilot because the agent should work with internal documents? Only then comes Copilot Studio. Many people believe they always need Premium. Often that is not true. So don't buy across the board for everyone, buy for the tasks that need it.

    1. Level 1

      Microsoft Copilot Chat without an add-on license

      Writes and rewrites, researches the web and analyzes what you paste or upload.

      Limit: Copilot Chat does not fetch your company's data on its own through Microsoft Graph. Only in Outlook does it include emails, calendar and meetings to a limited extent.

      Source: Learn: copilot/overview, microsoft-365-copilot-overview, as of October 15, 2026.

    2. Level 2

      Agent Builder without an add-on license

      You pack instructions and public websites into an agent that you use again and again.

      Limit: No access to your tenant's data. Agents with SharePoint content are billed by consumption and are off by default.

      Source: Learn: copilot/agents, copilot-studio-experience, as of October 15, 2026.

    3. Level 3

      License for Microsoft Copilot

      Microsoft 365 Copilot (Premium or Pro) adds broad access to organizational data through Graph and Work IQ, plus Priority Access in the apps. Availability depends on license and tenant configuration.

      Limit: According to Microsoft, Agent Builder fits when instructions and supported knowledge sources are enough, including for several departments through sharing and the org catalog. Microsoft names Copilot Studio for external channels such as a customer website, actions on external services, workflows and environments with release management.

      Source: Learn: microsoft-365-copilot-overview, copilot/agents, copilot-studio-experience, as of October 15, 2026.

    4. Level 4

      Copilot Studio

      Agents for the whole company or for external customers, with multistep processes, connectors to other systems and publishing on websites.

      Limit: In the trial you only build and check. Running agents consumes capacity in Copilot Credits.

      Source: Learn: copilot-studio-experience, requirements-licensing-subscriptions, requirements-licensing, as of October 15, 2026.

    Eleven tasks from everyday work in small businesses

    Tap a task to see why this level is enough. Seven of eleven work without an add-on license.

    • Write texts following your style guide, with the Prompt Coach agentLevel 1: Copilot Chat

      Enough, because Microsoft counts the Prompt Coach agent among the agents included at no extra cost in Microsoft Copilot Chat. You find it at m365.cloud.microsoft under Agents, then All agents. If it is not there, your IT may not have enabled it. According to Microsoft, uploading files and dictation are part of Copilot Chat.

      My best example. This is how I do it, it is my experience and not a Microsoft procedure:

      1. Sign in with your work account, not your personal one. Only then does the protection for company data apply (green shield).
      2. Open Prompt Coach and upload your style guide or your guidelines, for example on the right form of address or on gender-inclusive language. If the upload does not work, simply copy the content into the chat.
      3. Ask it for a prompt that follows these guidelines, and save the prompt.
      4. After that you just write or dictate away and get the text the way your style guide wants it. You copy it into the email, the document or the publication.

      Good to know: Prompt Coach reads your guidelines once, when it creates the prompt, not again every time you use it. A style guide rarely changes, so this saves a lot of time. If it does change, you have the prompt created again.

      Source: Learn: m365-agents-admin-guide (Prompt Coach at no extra cost, path via Agents) and copilot/faq (file upload, dictation), as of October 15, 2026. Related technique: No. 11 The Shadow Writer

    • Draft a reply to an angry customer emailLevel 1: Copilot Chat

      Enough, because according to Microsoft, Copilot Chat drafts and rewrites content. You paste the customer email as text.

      Source: Learn: copilot/overview, as of October 15, 2026. Technique: Draft a reply to a difficult customer email

    • Turn meeting notes into minutes with action itemsLevel 1: Copilot Chat

      Enough, because you paste the notes yourself. Copilot Chat processes what you bring in, even without a license.

      Source: Learn: microsoft-365-copilot-overview, as of October 15, 2026. Technique: Turn notes into minutes with action items

    • Research suppliers on the web, with sourcesLevel 1: Copilot Chat

      Enough, because Copilot Chat grounds its answers in web search through Bing. Your IT can turn off web search.

      Source: Learn: copilot/privacy-and-protections, as of October 15, 2026, copilot/manage, as of October 13, 2026. Technique: No. 12 The Trail

    • Compare three quotes as PDFsLevel 1: Copilot Chat

      Enough, because uploading and analyzing files is included in Copilot Chat. The upload has limits, Microsoft gives no numbers for them.

      Source: Learn: copilot/overview, as of October 15, 2026. Technique: No. 9 The Quote Duel

    • Summarize last week's emails about a projectLevel 1: Copilot Chat in Outlook

      Enough, because according to Microsoft, Copilot Chat in Outlook includes your emails, calendar, meetings, chats and a limited set of files even without a license. Outside Outlook, you bring the email in yourself.

      Source: Learn: copilot/overview and copilot/privacy-and-protections, as of October 15, 2026.

    • Agent that answers questions about your own websiteLevel 2: Agent Builder

      Enough, because agents with instructions and public websites are included at no extra cost, and your website is public. The agent runs in Copilot Chat, not on your website.

      Source: Learn: copilot/agents, as of October 15, 2026. Technique: No. 15 The Gatekeeper

    • Agent that answers from internal guides on SharePointLevel 3: License or metered billing

      Needs level 3, because without a license, agents with SharePoint content are off by default and billed by consumption. With a license for Microsoft Copilot, they run with no admin action. If it is about a single document, you upload it at level 1.

      Source: Learn: copilot/agents, as of October 15, 2026. Technique for a single document: No. 13 The Dojo Quiz

    • Use Copilot directly in Word or ExcelApp access

      Copilot Chat (Basic) has no access to Copilot in Word, Excel, PowerPoint and OneNote without an add-on. Microsoft 365 Copilot (Basic) provides standard access in these apps without an add-on, which may be limited at high demand. Microsoft 365 Copilot (Premium or Pro) provides Priority Access and full functionality with an add-on license, including broad organizational data through Graph and Work IQ. The in-product label shows your level; availability depends on your license and tenant configuration.

      Source: Learn: microsoft-365-copilot-overview and copilot/overview, as of October 15, 2026. Details: row Word, Excel, PowerPoint

    • Agent that answers customers on your websiteLevel 4: Copilot Studio

      Needs level 4, because for external customers and publishing on websites, Microsoft names Copilot Studio. Running it consumes Copilot Credits. Publishing needs capacity (Copilot Credits or pay-as-you-go) and author permission.

      Source: Learn: copilot-studio-experience and requirements-licensing, as of October 15, 2026. Technique: No. 23 The Credit Compass

    • Agent that takes a request and creates a ticket from itLevel 4: Copilot Studio

      Needs level 4, because Microsoft assigns agents that create tickets or trigger processes to Copilot Studio. According to the rate table, an Agent Action costs 5 Copilot Credits.

      Source: Learn: copilot-studio-experience and requirements-messages-management, as of October 15, 2026. Technique: Agent that sorts and hands over requests

    Dojo rule 3

    Use what you already have first.

    If Microsoft Copilot Chat is included in your subscription, the equipment is already there. What is missing is practice.       Ready stance
      Write prompts with goal, context, source and expectations, and let yourself be interviewed.
    2. Yellow belt
      Second look
      Request and review drafts so that mistakes show up before you send them.

      Tracking
      Analyze files and web sources, every statement with where it was found.

    Frequently asked questions

    Short questions, documented answers.

    The answer comes first, the source at the end. Every answer shows its date.

    Is Microsoft Copilot Chat free?

    For eligible users, yes, at no extra cost. The conditions are signing in with a work or school account and a qualifying Microsoft 365 subscription in your organization.

    Source: Learn: copilot/manage, as of October 13, 2026. Details: qualifying subscriptions.

    Is a work account (Entra) enough for Copilot Chat?

    No. An Entra account alone is not an entitlement, your organization needs a qualifying subscription.

    A personal Microsoft account, for example from Outlook.com, is something else. According to Microsoft, the enterprise protections do not apply with it.

    Source: Learn: copilot/manage, as of October 13, 2026.

    Can Copilot Chat read my emails and files without a license?

    Not automatically through Microsoft Graph. You bring in content yourself, as text or by file upload. According to Microsoft, Copilot Chat in Outlook can include emails, calendar, meetings, chats and a limited set of files without a license.

    Source: Learn: copilot/overview and microsoft-365-copilot-overview, as of October 13, 2026. Details: access to your tenant's data.

    Is Copilot in Word and Excel free?

    Copilot Chat (Basic) has no access to Copilot in Word, Excel, PowerPoint and OneNote without an add-on. Microsoft 365 Copilot (Basic) provides standard access in these apps without an add-on, which may be limited at high demand. Microsoft 365 Copilot (Premium or Pro) provides Priority Access and full functionality with an add-on license, including broad organizational data through Graph and Work IQ. The in-product label shows your level; availability depends on your license and tenant configuration.

    According to Microsoft, Microsoft Copilot Chat itself does have agents for Word, Excel and PowerPoint that create documents, spreadsheets and presentations directly in the chat. According to Microsoft, which of these are available to you depends on settings and license.

    Source: Learn: copilot/overview, as of October 13, 2026. Agents in the chat: copilot/faq and copilot/manage, as of October 15, 2026.

    Can I try Copilot Studio for free?

    Yes, as an individual with a work or school account. You can build agents and check them in the test chat, but you cannot publish them. Personal email addresses are rejected.

    Source: Learn: requirements-licensing-subscriptions, as of October 13, 2026.

    Are agents in Copilot Chat free?

    Agents that rely only on instructions and public websites are included at no extra cost. Agents with data from SharePoint or Graph connectors are billed by consumption and are off by default for Copilot Chat users.

    Source: Learn: copilot/agents, as of October 13, 2026.

    How much does a Copilot Credit cost?

    This page does not give the price, because I have not checked it. Microsoft publishes consumption rates per feature, for example 1 Copilot Credit for a Classic Answer and 2 for a Generative Answer. The rates can change.

    Source: Learn: requirements-messages-management, as of October 13, 2026. Costs and license exemptions: powerplatformgovernance.com (in German).

    Do users of a published agent need their own license?

    No. Running it does consume capacity of your tenant, though.

    Source: Learn: requirements-licensing, as of October 13, 2026.

    Are my prompts used for training?

    According to Microsoft, prompts and responses are not used to train foundation models. Web search queries run separately through Bing under their own data rules. This is Microsoft's statement and not an assessment of your case.

    Source: Learn: copilot/faq and enterprise-data-protection, as of October 13, 2026.

    How do I recognize that enterprise protection is active?

    By the green shield next to the button for a new chat (“New Chat”). According to Microsoft, Enterprise Data Protection then applies to your prompts and responses.

    Source: Learn: copilot/privacy-and-protections, section “Enterprise data protection for prompts and responses”, and copilot/manage, as of October 15, 2026.
