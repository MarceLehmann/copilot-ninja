    The Invisibility CloakPseudonymise text before it goes anywhere

    Before a real case moves on

    The situation

    You want to discuss a real case in a training session, with an external party or in a web search. Names, amounts and numbers must not go along.

    The cut

    Before

    “Pseudonymise this text.”

    After

    Make it yours

    Nothing leaves your browser.

    e.g. yes or no
    e.g. the text with the real case

    Prompt to copy

    Goal: Pseudonymise people, companies and places in the text below with consistent placeholders. The content should stay understandable.

Source: Only the text below. Do not add anything.

Expectations: Replace
- names of people with [PERSON_1], [PERSON_2] and so on
- companies and organizations with [COMPANY_1] and so on
- addresses and places with [PLACE_1], phone numbers and email addresses with [CONTACT_1], customer, contract and invoice numbers with [NUMBER_1]
- amounts with [AMOUNT_1], unless I write below that they may stay
The same people and companies always get the same placeholder.
Output:
1. The pseudonymised text.
2. Separately below it, a table “Mapping” with placeholder and original. I keep this table to myself.
3. A list “Check” with passages that could still make a person recognizable, for example an unusual job title, a date or a special event.

Amounts stay: [Amounts stay, e.g. yes or no]

Text:
[Case text, e.g. the text with the real case]

    No add-on license in Microsoft Copilot Chat. Without a copy button: select the text and copy it with Ctrl+C.

    How to use it

    1. Paste the text, decide whether amounts stay, and send.
    2. Work through the “Check” list and rewrite those passages yourself.
    3. Only pass on the pseudonymised part, never the mapping.
    4. When an answer comes back, you put the originals back in yourself.

    How to spot a good answer

    • A readable text with consistent placeholders.
    • A mapping table, separate from the text.
    • A list of remaining risks under “Check”.

    The check

    Give the pseudonymised text to a colleague who does not know the case. Count how many people or companies she still guesses. The goal is zero.

    Limits

    • There is no certainty that nothing recognizable remains. The “Check” list is a hint, you read it yourself.
    • The mapping is in the same chat. According to Microsoft, prompts and responses under Enterprise Data Protection are logged and retained and are available for audit and eDiscovery. So do not share the chat.
    • Only paste the original text if your internal rules allow it. Microsoft states Enterprise Data Protection for prompts and responses when you sign in with a work or school account.
    • Pseudonymising does not replace checking with the person responsible for data protection in your organization.
    • Strictly speaking, you are pseudonymising: the mapping lets each person be identified again. Do not release the text just because names have been replaced.

    Sources

    Microsoft Learn: Enterprise data protection in Microsoft Copilot and Microsoft Copilot Chat, Microsoft Learn: Frequently asked questions about Microsoft Copilot Chat. As of October 15, 2026.
