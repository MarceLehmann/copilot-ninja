    The ReconnaissanceBriefing dossier before a meeting

    Before a customer meeting

    • Belt Yellow belt, level 2
    • Type Brushstroke
    • Access With a license for Microsoft Copilot
    • Try it approx. 5 min

    The situation

    Tomorrow you meet the customer you have been exchanging emails, chats and minutes with for months. Who committed to what is scattered across many places.

    The cut

    Before

    “Summarize everything about Muster AG for me.”

    After

    Make it yours

    Nothing leaves your browser.

    e.g. title of the meeting
    e.g. person or company
    e.g. the last three months

    Prompt to copy

    Goal: Prepare me for the meeting [Meeting, e.g. title of the meeting] on [Date] with [Counterpart, e.g. person or company].

Source: My emails, chats, meetings and files about [Counterpart, e.g. person or company] from [Period, e.g. the last three months].

Expectations:
1. Where things stand, in three sentences.
2. Open commitments: what I promised and what the other side promised, each with date and source.
3. Topics that stayed open at the last contact.
4. Two questions I can use to open the meeting.
Mark every detail that is based on only a single source.

    With a license for Microsoft Copilot. Without a copy button: select the text and copy it with Ctrl+C.

    How to use it

    1. Open it in Microsoft Copilot with a license, fill in the fields, send.
    2. Compare the open commitments with the linked sources.
    3. Confirm details that rest on only one source before the meeting, or leave them out.
    4. Without a license: upload the most important emails or minutes as files and change the “Source” line to “the attached files”.

    How to spot a good answer

    • Where things stand, in three sentences.
    • Commitments from both sides with date and source.
    • Marked details that rest on only one source.
    • Two opening questions.

    The check

    Measure the preparation time per customer meeting, for five meetings with and five meetings without the dossier.

    Limits

    • According to Microsoft, only licensed Microsoft Copilot automatically uses emails, chats, meetings and files through Microsoft Graph. Microsoft Copilot Chat without a license does not.
    • According to Microsoft, the answer only sees content you have access to.
    • Verbal agreements without a note are missing from the dossier.

    Sources

    Microsoft Learn: What is Microsoft Copilot?, Microsoft Learn: Overview of Microsoft Copilot Chat. As of October 15, 2026.
