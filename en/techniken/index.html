    The threshold

    From here on you build agents.

    In the Copilot Studio trial you can create an agent and check it in the test chat, but not publish it. Publishing needs capacity (Copilot Credits or pay-as-you-go) and author permission. (Microsoft Learn, as of October 13, 2026). All agent techniques are free here, no sign-up.

    If you prefer to practice with guidance: the Power Platform Academy runs the live course “Copilot Ninja in 4 Wochen” (in German). The course has its own curriculum and does not follow the belts of this site. The next dates are not set yet, you can join the waiting list on the course page.
