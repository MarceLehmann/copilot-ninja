    The threshold

    From here on you build agents.

    In the Copilot Studio trial you can create an agent and check it in the test chat, but not publish it (Microsoft Learn, as of October 13, 2026). All agent techniques are free here, no sign-up.

    If you prefer to practice with guidance: the Power Platform Academy runs the live course “Copilot Ninja in 4 Wochen” (in German), dates open, registering interest is non-binding. The course has its own curriculum and does not follow the belts of this site. To the course page

    Four-week path for self-study

    Through all belts in four weeks

    A free plan, no sign-up and no course. One or two belts each week.

    1. 1

      Week 1

      WhiteYellow

      If you practice this week’s techniques, afterwards you have:

      • the questions you have to answer yourself before an important text (No. 1)
      • your recipient’s objections, while you can still fix them (No. 5)
      • a list of differences between draft and source (No. 6)
      • the reasons your project could fail (No. 7)
    2. 2

      Week 2

      Orange

      If you practice this week’s techniques, afterwards you have:

      • a comparison of quotes with the fine print in one table (No. 9)
      • a list of deadlines for your calendar entries (No. 10)
      • your writing style profile (No. 11)
      • a quiz on a policy, with references (No. 13)
    3. 3

      Week 3

      GreenBlue

      If you practice this week’s techniques, afterwards you have:

      • your own agent built from your best chat (No. 14)
      • a review table for its instructions (No. 17)
      • 20 test questions with the list “not covered” (No. 18)
      • a safeguard block for every instruction (No. 19)
    4. 4

      Week 4

      BrownBlack

      If you practice this week’s techniques, afterwards you have:

      • a template to pass on real cases anonymized (No. 22)
      • an estimate of the order of magnitude of Copilot Credits your agent uses (No. 23)
      • an operations sheet with shutdown criteria and a responsible person (No. 24)
      • a routine for the monthly check (No. 25)

    After four weeks you have your own agent with test questions, safeguard block, credit estimate and operations sheet.

    What you need for it is shown in the access line of every technique.
