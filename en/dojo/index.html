    What happens next

    • Every submission is read and checked against the documentation on Microsoft Learn.
    • Only what passes this check is added. Unchecked content never appears, not even as “under review”.
    • You are only named if you explicitly say yes.
    • By submitting, you allow your contribution to be published in the Dojo after it has been checked, shortened and adapted. What happens to your email is described in the Privacy page.
    • No customer, personal or company data in the prompt.
